# Negative CLASS normalization result (2026-11-25)

The energy-closed workflow [45623866786](https://github.com/IskanderAbdullin/Dark-Matter/actions/runs/45623866786) succeeded computationally, with two kinetic meshes agreeing in the apparent half-power crossing within 0.8%. **This is not a physical half-power measurement:** the large-scale ratio P_kin/P_CDM was ~18 at k=.01 Mpc^-1 and ~6.8 at .1 Mpc^-1. A nearly cold species plus a hot component cannot be treated as a validated adiabatic equal-density model with such a large long-wave normalization discrepancy. Varying fluid trigger and disabling the fluid approximation changes small-scale results but leaves the giant large-scale discrepancy.

Local integration-by-parts tests on the reconstructed PSD and transformed 240-point quadrature give derivatives -2.99887,-3.99917,-4.99954 for cold and -3.00001,-4.00002,-5.00005 for hot (expected -3,-4,-5). This rules out a gross error in the local smooth derivative integrals, not a bug in CLASS's derivative routine or perturbation initialization. Check gauges, replace cold numerical tracer with matched true CDM on scales where its free-streaming is negligible, change quadrature strategy and inspect background/transfer amplitudes. The new diagnostic workflow does not yet assert any physical result.

The earlier external-bath apparent half-power result [45622816489](https://github.com/IskanderAbdullin/Dark-Matter/actions/runs/45622816489) is likewise **provisional** pending this normalization control. Abundance and kinetic stress-energy results do not depend on CLASS.
