## Exact angular reduction
Let E_i=q_i², S=E1+E2=E3+E4. P=|q1+q2|, Rrel=|q1−q2|; B=P Rrel/2. Outgoing E3 is uniform on [S/2−B,S/2+B]. Relative speed times outgoing density is 1/P. Since dc=P dP/(q1q2), angular integral yields ΔP/(2q1q2). Allowed P interval is intersection of [|q1−q2|,q1+q2] and [|q3−q4|,q3+q4], width ΔP=2 min(q_i). Including q1²dq1 q2²dq2 yields symmetric energy kernel W=min(q_i)/4 with dE1 dE2 dE3. Pointwise C1=(1/(2q1))∫ dE2 dE3 min(q_i) [gain−loss]. This is the established isotropic Nordheim min-energy kernel (see https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022123623004469 and conservative/entropic discretization context https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.2748), derived here with the normalization of this benchmark; not a new general kernel.
Compared independent 2D (n48) to original 3D (n32) for linear psi=1,3,3.9,3.999999 and 5 velocities each: typical relative differences <3e−4; at gap 1e−6 q=.3 and 1 differences .13%/.69%, indicating angular quadrature sensitivity near critical cancellation. At q=0 independent equality to ~1e−14 except extreme gap 2.18e−7 relative.
Separate stimulated gain/loss divided by f at q=0: at gap .1, linear 10.0323/9.90232 per Gyr, quadratic 9.73891/9.54152; at gap 1e−4, 12578.70/12578.52 and 13580.58/13580.22; at gap 1e−6, 1258941.108/1258940.923 and 1360377.016/1360376.649. Gross rates approximately inverse-gap, while net finite. Direct cubic-cancelled evaluation important. Gross rates are not automatically net relaxation eigenvalues or a proof of kinetic breakdown.

## Conditional local thermodynamic saturation
For an isolated homogeneous cell conserving local dimensionless number N=∫q²F dq and kinetic E=∫q⁴F dq, saturated BE at same E has β=[Γ(5/2)ζ(5/2)/(2E)]^(2/5), Ncrit=Γ(3/2)ζ(3/2)/(2β^(3/2)). Critical linear central N/Ncrit=1.1173655; threshold ψ=3.9934363 at r=.204466R, enclosed halo fraction 9.88228e−5; integrating local excess yields 2.60172e−6 of total halo. Quadratic central ratio 1.1895268; threshold ψ=3.9854576, r=.310122R, enclosed fraction 3.26281e−4, conditional excess 1.30798e−5. For δ=0.001 both remain supercritical; δ=.01 linear is subcritical (ratio .97582), quadratic still supercritical (1.02824, excess 6.50e−7); δ=.02 both subcritical. These are conditional local equilibrium numbers only, not bounds or predictions for a real halo exchanging particles/energy between cells; actual condensate requires coupled transport and C12/zero-mode physics.
