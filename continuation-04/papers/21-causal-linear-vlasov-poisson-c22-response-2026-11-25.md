# 21. Causal linearized Vlasov–Poisson response to the signed C22 source
2026-11-25. Critical linear and quadratic lowered Bose benchmarks, same illustrative parameters as papers 19–20. No claim of nonlinear physical halo evolution.

## Method
With L0 the unperturbed Liouville operator, solve (∂t+L0)δf=C22[f0]+∇δΦ·∇v f0, ∇²δΦ=4πG δρ. The force source is odd in radial velocity and generated by independent equilibrium tracers for each radial quadrature node. In dimensionless variables v_r=√2 q μ and δQ=Qt δM (δM relative to total mass), the signed source weight per unit δM is −dr q²dq F'(s) v_r dμ/(Ic TIME), with μ measure normalized on [-1,1]. The r² phase-volume factor cancels the radial force denominator. q=√(η−ψ) tan θ resolves the near-critical susceptibility; independent integration by parts gives I'(ψ)=∫F dq/2. The causal matrix kernel K is streamed in the original potential, and enclosed perturbation d(t)=Born(t)+∫K(t−τ)d(τ)dτ is solved with the zero endpoints K(0)=d(0)=0. Smooth Gaussian probes exp(−r²/2ℓ²) are evaluated separately from field sampling. No C22'[f0]δf (higher order in cross section for finite time), no evolving background or condensation.
Context: sourced halo perturbation theory https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/368/4/1657/1155285 ; independent response-matrix approach https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10630 . These are methodological precedents, not validation of this solver.

## Critical negative result
Raw pointwise field at tiny radial nodes is not converged (max dimensionless raw force 3.44/386 at 1 Gyr), despite broad-probe agreement and exact global mass. Discrete signed tracer cancellation and near-zero enclosed denominator make central results unsafe. Volume-balanced windows alone do not fix it. A diagnostic projection δM(r<r_a)=δM(r_a)(r/r_a)^p for the force only, with r_a the first node at/above .05/.1/.2R and p=2 or 3, is NOT a physical closure. At requested .1R, p=3, actual anchors .15348/.15321R, max effective force .0183/.0203 at 1 Gyr; broad central Gaussian probes change by <0.007% vs unguarded at that time. This establishes sensitivity of broad observables to that tested numerical defect, not convergence of central density, stability of a condensate, or accuracy of the whole linear model. Resolve the center via an independent orbit-projected operator or conservative smooth basis; then evaluate evolving C22 and nonlinear phase-space positivity.
