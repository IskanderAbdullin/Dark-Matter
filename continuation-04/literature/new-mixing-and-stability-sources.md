# Новые источники: phase-space mixing и регулярность kinetic cutoff

Это критическое тематическое дополнение, не заявления о полном прочтении всех источников.

1. Tremaine, Hénon & Lynden-Bell, H-functions and mixing in violent relaxation, MNRAS 219 (1986) 285–297, https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/219.2.285. Общие смешивающие функционалы phase-space.
2. Dehnen, Phase-space mixing and the merging of cusps, MNRAS 360 (2005) 892, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2005.09099.x; https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0504246. Excess-mass D(F) monotonicity under coarse-grained mixing, used in paper 10.
3. Hadžić, Rein, Schrecker, Straub, Damping Versus Oscillations for a Gravitational Vlasov–Poisson System, Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis 249 (2025) 45, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-025-02114-y. Full open text examined. For THEIR radial central-point-mass and fixed-angular-momentum model, k>1 boundary polytropic exponent leads damping while 1/2<k<=1 has undamped modes. Our 3D isotropic Bose halo does NOT meet theorem assumptions. Motivates testing the C1 smooth Bose cutoff in paper 12.
4. On the local uniqueness of steady states for the Vlasov–Poisson system (2024), Journal of Functional Analysis, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2024.110347. Stability/equimeasurable classes require specified phase-space constraints.
5. Steady states of the spherically symmetric Vlasov–Poisson system as fixed points of a mass-preserving algorithm, Nonlinear Analysis RWA (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nonrwa.2025.104467. Independent numerical construction benchmark for future global VP problem.
6. Stability Problems in Collisionless Kinetic Systems, invited ICM 2026 review, https://doi.org/10.1137/25M1805825. Overview of global linear/nonlinear stability issues and limitations of simple local criteria.

Main methodological message: nonlinear stability, Landau damping, fine-grained equimeasurability, coarse-grained mixing, thermodynamic kinetic equilibration and observational inference are distinct questions. Neither a positive Bose DF nor a virial identity alone solves them.
