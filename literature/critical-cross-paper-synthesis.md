# Focused cross-paper synthesis: decisive checks for the 2021 noncondensed Bose halo
**Research date:** 2026-11-21. **Evidence status:** cited publisher abstracts/landing pages and supplied source-paper text/BibTeX. This is a targeted critical reading of claims and applicability, not 100 independent line-by-line full-text reads.

The broad \`100-screened.md\` inventory is **100 distinct records**. This separate document prioritizes source assumptions and implications in the strongest directly adjacent works, including a few *newly found studies beyond that fixed 100-item baseline*.

## 1. Slepian & Goodman (2012), repulsive boson dark matter in thermal equilibrium
[Published article](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21901.x) / [arXiv:1109.3844](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.3844).

- **Source claim:** the two constraints — required observationally viable galaxy rotation curves and the upper self-interaction bound from a merging cluster — cannot simultaneously be met by the *particular model* of repulsive bosonic dark matter in **thermal equilibrium**.
- **Implication for Abdullin–Popov:** it is essential to show whether the mostly noncondensed 2021 halo differs in the specific parameter assumptions, repulsive interaction, collisional thermalization and condensate geometry that drive this no-go.
- **Not justified:** claiming that a two-component, nonthermal, collisionless, or nonstandard-production 86 eV model is excluded *solely* by the Slepian–Goodman abstract.
- **Required calculation:** put both halo solutions in common variables \(m,\sigma/m,\nu,T,f_{\rm BS}\), obtain their collision rates and compute actual rotation curves with the Slepian–Goodman approximations as a test case.

## 2. Madsen (1990), bosonic phase-space bounds
[PRL 64, 2744](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.64.2744).

- **Source claim:** extends the Tremaine–Gunn phase-space reasoning beyond fermions, including bosonic dark matter.
- **Implication:** a bosonic interpretation does not inherit *fermionic* Pauli bounds. Nevertheless, finite phase-space density, occupation, source abundance and chemical potentials are part of the physical consistency test.
- **Required calculation:** infer \(n\lambda_{\rm dB}^3\), occupancy and conserved charge, then compare with a *specified* cosmological initial distribution through Liouville/kinetics, not a one-line universal mass bound.

## 3. Chavanis (2019), solitonic core plus isothermal envelope
[PRD 100, 083022](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.100.083022).

- **Source claim:** constructs a predictive BEC dark-matter core–halo model with a solitonic core and isothermal atmosphere, including regimes of halo mass with distinct core–envelope behavior.
- **Implication:** the combination of an equilibrium envelope and a compact quantum core is **not by itself a new idea**; novelty must instead derive from a quantitatively different phase, independently predicted condensate population, critical profile effect, or stronger observational test.
- **Required comparison:** common total halo mass, finite outer boundary, physical core radius, core mass fraction and observational data. Independent per-galaxy \(R,V_0,\nu,\alpha,\gamma\) can hide weak identifiability.

## 4. Abdullin & Popov (2019), opposite phase-dominance limit
[Russian journal text](https://www.stfi.ru/journal/STFI_2019_01/STFI_2019_01_Abdullin.pdf).

- **Source claim:** in the earlier large condensed-core setup, the nondegenerate envelope can contribute insignificantly to rotation curves when core and halo sizes are comparable.
- **Implication:** the 2021 mostly noncondensed regime is a *different physical limit*, not a trivial extension of the earlier condensate-dominated work.
- **Required bridge:** map dimensionless central condensate fraction, core-to-halo radius ratio and total condensate mass continuously between the two regimes; characterize bifurcations and whether either branch is stable.

## 5. Levkov, Panin & Tkachev (2018), gravitational condensation kinetics
[PRL 121, 151301](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.151301).

- **Source claim:** a kinetic mechanism can produce Bose–Einstein condensates/Bose stars through gravitational interactions in virialized dark-matter systems.
- **Implication:** a proposed population of Bose stars needs an actual formation time and mass function. It is not sufficient to place an isothermal Bose-star density into Jeans's equation.
- **Domain caveat:** the kinetic approximation may require high occupation and wave behavior; map the **50–150 eV gas** into its domain rather than transferring ultralight-mass scalings.
- **Required calculation:** compare \(t_{\rm cond}\) with halo age and dynamical time using actual densities, occupations and number-changing processes.

## 6. Chavanis (2025), condensation and distinct relaxation mechanisms
[2025 review](https://doi.org/10.3389/fspas.2025.1538434).

- **Source claim:** carefully distinguishes secular kinetic Bose condensation from faster gravitational cooling / collisionless violent relaxation, and reviews core–halo mass relations.
- **Implication:** collisionless relaxation is *not* automatically thermalization to a Bose–Einstein chemical potential; these are independent physical claims in the original model.
- **Required calculation:** test collision operators, detailed balance, entropy production and relaxation times for any proposed mechanism.

## 7. Levkov, Panin & Tkachev (2025), self-similar late-stage condensation
[PRD 112, 123533 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/52sq-jslx).

- **Source claim:** a kinetic self-similar attractor is relevant to Bose-star growth in some dark-matter cosmologies.
- **Implication:** the Bose-star mass function could be a dynamical prediction instead of independently fitted \(\alpha,\gamma\).
- **Domain caveat:** no numerical growth law or rate transfers to 86 eV without the full momentum/occupation/interaction mapping.

## 8. Wave-kinetic multi-component and self-interacting condensation (2023–2024)
[2023, PRD 108 043535](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.043535);
[2024, PRD 109 016002](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.016002).

- **Source claim:** develops kinetic formation/nucleation calculations in multi-component and interacting wave dark matter.
- **Implication:** the user’s two-component hypothesis has possible microscopic kinetic precedents, but a **thermal gas plus individual 86 eV condensate stars** differs sharply from ultralight coherent wave fields.
- **Required calculation:** derive the dominant collision integral and a justified diffusion/nucleation approximation for this particle mass and phase-space occupancy.

## 9. New 2026 interaction-range dependence
[Yukawa-screened Bose-star condensation, PRD 114 043519 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/g3dd-t8mw).

- **Source claim:** finite-range Yukawa screening modifies infrared relaxation and hence both condensate structure and formation time in the studied wave-dark-matter model.
- **Implication:** it is unsafe to treat condensation as depending only on integrated \(\sigma/m\); the momentum-transfer dependence or interaction range may strongly affect relaxation.
- **Required calculation:** if the 86 eV boson has a mediator, calculate \(d\sigma/d\Omega\), kinetic relaxation, and astrophysical scales of the interaction.

## 10. Rogers et al. (2024) and mixed relics (2025): cosmic structure
[2024 Lyman-alpha, PRD 109 043511](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.043511);
[2025 mixed warm dark matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06367).

- **Source claim:** the 2024 Lyman-alpha forest analysis reports 95%CL \(m_{\rm thermal\,WDM}>5.7\,{\rm keV}\) under its modeling choices, reduced to 4.1 keV under some narrower matched scales/priors. Mixed warm+cold matter constraints depend on the warm fraction and primordial distribution.
- **Implication:** an **86 eV freely streaming thermal relic** is cosmologically suspect. Nevertheless, neither the 5.7-keV figure nor its 4.1-keV sensitivity is a universal boson mass bound.
- **Required calculation:** specify cosmological distribution \(f_0(q)\), entropy and chemical potential history, condensate fraction \(f_c(a)\); compute CMB+linear \(T(k,z)\), then Lyman-alpha forward models. No viable window has been demonstrated here.

## 11. SPARC catalog and 2026 cross-model fits
[Official SPARC database](https://astroweb.cwru.edu/SPARC/);
[official mass model plain text](https://astroweb.cwru.edu/SPARC/MassModels_Lelli2016c.mrt);
[2026 SPARC model degeneracy study](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag2148).

- **Source claim:** official SPARC provides 175 disk galaxies with gas and stellar rotation contributions. The 2026 comparative study fits 147 SPARC galaxies using CDM/baryonic feedback, WDM, fuzzy DM and MOND, emphasizing cross-model degeneracies.
- **Implication:** a small chi-squared on 20 dark-matter-dominated galaxies does not establish a unique boson mass, especially if the same particle has to reproduce a much larger range of baryon fractions.
- **Required calculation:** reproduce the exact source 22-galaxy sample with observational covariance; analyze SPARC with \(\nu_i\) physically tied to **one** mass \(m\), inclination/distance and mass-to-light nuisances, and held-out galaxies. The four unit tests in the repository are not substitute SPARC fits.

## 12. Extended-object astrometric lensing (2026)
[Bhandari et al., JCAP09(2026)072](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2026/09/072).

- **Source claim:** presents an astrometric microlensing study of extended dark objects including boson-star lenses.
- **Implication:** a Bose-star radius alone, without object mass, abundance, distance and survey efficiency, cannot be converted into a microlensing exclusion.
- **Required calculation:** compute \(R_{\rm BS}/R_E\), extended-lens astrometric signals and selection-weighted counts integrated over \(dN/dM\).

## Four independent veto tests
1. **Cosmic structure veto:** no physically produced 50–150 eV phase-space distribution passes CMB/relic abundance/early-structure tests.
2. **Thermalization veto:** relaxation to the assumed BE distribution is too slow or contradicts the required self-interaction regime.
3. **Population veto:** no dynamical formation and survival mechanism can produce the condensate fraction needed by galaxy kinematics.
4. **Kinematic veto:** joint-mass baryon-aware held-out rotation curves cannot recover the reported 86 eV scale with a reasonable predictive error budget.

**Research ordering:** (1) cosmological production and streaming, (2) thermodynamic/kinetic viability, (3) original rotation-curve error bar recovery, (4) full SPARC hierarchical inference, (5) compact-object lensing. If a P0 veto is confirmed, modify or reject the model rather than adding freely tuned fit parameters.
