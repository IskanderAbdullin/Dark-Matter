# 2025–2026 additional cross-paper screen: particle-number, equilibration and extended-lens observables

These **seven** published sources supplement the 44-reference annotated inventory in critical-theory-crosswalk.md (51 total entries in continuation-03, not asserting 51 full-text readings).

45. Chemical and kinetic equilibrium in cosmology: Facts and myths, PRD 112 103509 (2025), https://doi.org/10.1103/lfvd-bkk4. Distinguishes chemical abundance vs kinetic shape. **Critical conceptual challenge** to assigning a single global \(\mu,T\) from virialization.
46. Guzmán, Castellanos & Mastache, Equilibrium halo solutions of the Gross–Pitaevskii–Poisson system: the role of the particle number, EPJC 86 1103 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-16373-w. Retains N in interacting/quantum-pressure regimes. **Critical check** of pure TF mass-independent star radius outside TF.
47. Dror et al., Indirect detection of hot dark matter, JHEP05(2025)127, https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP05(2025)127. Hot eV relics around halos; different candidate masses / abundance than source.
48. Bhandari et al., Probing extended dark objects through astrometric microlensing from Gaia, JCAP09(2026)072, https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2026/09/072. Extended boson-star lensing, Gaia forecasts; relevant at corrected ~133AU but event rates require population model.
49. Subaru-HSC through a different lens: Microlensing by extended dark matter structures, PRD 102 083021 (2020), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.102.083021. Finite lens profile and point-lens selection differences.
50. Microlensing signatures of extended dark objects using machine learning, PRD 109 123004 (2024), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.123004. Modeling and classification strategy for new observational pipeline.
51. Periodic Gravitational Lensing with Oscillating Boson Stars, PRL 137 031403 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1103/6w3p-8ym2. Oscillating compact solutions, **not automatically** applicable to static TF Bose stars.

**Verification depth:** publisher summaries/abstracts, bibliographic records and selected open explanatory sections, not seven new full PDF reads. No original observation/event likelihood carried out.

**Decision impacts:** article 46 motivates explicit N and gradient energy before assigning a fixed object radius; article 45 prevents conflating decoupling and virial thermalization; 48–50 supply the proper observable machinery for a mass/radius/abundance population; 51 flags an alternate class of coherent objects rather than an assured signature of the original model.
