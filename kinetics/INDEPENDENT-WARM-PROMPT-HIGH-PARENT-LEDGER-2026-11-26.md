could not equate new tail births to gross reacting use. Test
the **gross inverse-decay source** into (p_Hge1) GeV on the
isolated prompt trajectory that starts with the actual q32
archive and explicit vacuum out to q64. Use an independently
cut parent-energy integral and split the daughter angles at
their shell boundaries, including each (p=0.5) GeV tag.
These are physical momentum cuts in this fixed-clock calculation.

At the initial time the rate is exactly zero by support. At
(HDelta t=5	imes10^{-6}), the high-(H) inverse energy
rate divided by initial dark energy times (H) is
(3.80	imes10^{-32}); the tagged (ge0.5) GeV daughter
inputs supply (2.22	imes10^{-32}) in the same units.
The latter is over half of the high parent energy at each
measured frame, as required by the two-input threshold.
The frozen high-parent energy fraction is
(6.74	imes10^{-45}) at the terminal sample. These positive
figures follow the tiny explicitly populated distributions,
not an energy-only bound or a claim of zero above threshold.

Only (L+phi	o H) in the prompt model is measured. This
does not bound other heavy production, actual inherited
exterior occupation, reheating, expansion or repeated
use through the full cosmological epoch.
