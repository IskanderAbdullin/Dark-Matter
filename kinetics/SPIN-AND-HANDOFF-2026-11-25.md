## Exact multiplicity interpretation and next microphysical model

For two independent identical Majorana pairs indexed by a=1,2, each with spin multiplicity two, one shared scalar phi, identical initial distributions, masses, widths and symmetric SM rates, the identical-distribution subspace is invariant. The scalar collision RHS is the sum over pairs, 2*Cphi(single pair), while each fermion per-occupation RHS is unchanged. Total fermion energy and stress trace double, so the common H and bath energy exchange must use the sum. Numerically checked the dark RHS, weights and bath rates at x=.1,.3,2,10 to roundoff. Thus effective g=4 can describe TWO identical Majorana pairs without treating a one-pair Majorana table as a Dirac calculation. It is a model-building option, not a viable UV completion: coherent mixing, flavor-dependent rates and helicity treatment have been omitted. The nominal seesaw mapping of kappaSM1=47 yields ~.05 eV per parent, hence two independent directions would contribute ~.10 eV in total before additional states; oscillation splittings, cosmological sum constraints, and flavor/cancellation structure must be jointly addressed. Next implement unequal-pair parent/daughter kinetics or a symmetry-protected seesaw with appropriate helicity-resolved rates. Shift symmetry is essential for technical naturalness (Bonnefoy et al., PRL 130 111803, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.111803); an unprotected pseudoscalar Yukawa mass estimate is not a derived shift-symmetric finite-T self-energy.
