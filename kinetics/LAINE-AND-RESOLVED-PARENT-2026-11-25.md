## Momentum-resolved parent

A complete exact n=80 integration reached x=80, Omega=.112865, hot=.004013, energy residual 1.41e-8. The finer strict n=120 run reached x=7.758 under a 600-second cap (not a completed relic solution). The energy-preserving hybrid handoff at x=7.758 gives parent shape TV=6.16e-5, relative source mismatch .00391 and local spectral L1 mismatch .00145. Continue using number closure to x=80: Omega=.115815, hot=.003865. An early x=.3 handoff is a negative control: TV=.0642, same-grid FD source differs by factor 5.1, and handoff-to-closure source differs by factor 4.98. Thus early kinetic-equilibrium closure is demonstrably unreliable. Late switch is plausible but conditional until converged independent mesh and switch-time checks. The n=80 full result is only coarse; do not interpret either as a converged prediction. New solver tolerances and Radau option are explicit. A 48-cell validation for the table-resolved geometry failed the 15% spontaneous normalization tolerance at x=20 (19.3% deficit); use >=64 cells for that regression and independent refinement in research runs.
