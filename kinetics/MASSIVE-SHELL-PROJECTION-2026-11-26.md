## Result
For a constant occupation f_i in each fixed q=p/Tref shell, use epsilon=sqrt(q^2+mu^2), mu=m/Tref and Tref proportional to a^-1. Omit a common species prefactor g/(2*pi^2). The weights are the q integrals of q^2, q^2 epsilon, q^4/epsilon, and q^2 mu^2/epsilon for N, R, 3P and trace respectively. Both R-3P=trace and dR/dln a=trace at fixed occupation follow pointwise. More generally dR/dln a contains the additional sum of energy weights times df_i/dln a. See Ma & Bertschinger, ApJ 455, 7 (1995), Eq. 52, https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9506072 .

Implemented a momentum-space weight reference that still resolves the leading pressure when both float64 energy endpoints round to the rest energy. Massless weights are closed form; massive weights use common Gauss–Legendre nodes. Unsplit 32-point quadrature failed the initial trace-accuracy target (maximum relative discrepancy 4.90e-7); subdividing wide cells across the transition fixed it. In 1,605 deterministic cells, maximum relative trace discrepancy was 1.80e-10 against independent adaptive quadrature, and maximum relative energy discrepancy 1.82e-11. Maximum absolute log-differentiation residual relative to energy was 7.53e-11; algebraic residual relative to energy 4.37e-16. Details in the [shell moment evidence](results/massive-shell-moments-2026-11-26.json). These are numerical tests of shell moments, not of the evolution or of the two-mass collision area.

## First integration experiment
Use the momentum edges as authoritative. For both daughters keep g N_i f_i as shell number (up to the common normalization); a common signed pair source J_ij must supply identical number changes to its two daughters, independent of degeneracy. Replace the massless trace source in the energy-closed solver with parent plus both daughter traces. Use the massive shell energy means Ebar_i=R_i/N_i when measuring the energy actually added to each discrete daughter spectrum.

Separately instrument collision-energy bookkeeping: if the parent energy sink is evaluated from the continuous pair quadrature, compare it to the discrete daughter load sum J_ij*(Ebar_i+rbar_j), not just to the total continuity residual. A temperature equation can numerically cancel this difference without validating the physical transfer to the bath. A differential cell-energy bound follows from Ebar_i lying between its energy edges: for an absolute pair-event measure the mismatch is at most the integral of (Delta E_i+Delta r_j) against its total variation. Do not use |J_ij| for that bound when signed gain and loss cancel within a cell. Run mesh refinement to quantify residual, and either implement conservative remapping or explicitly document/test its error before interpreting a new PSD. A momentum grid that fixes shell moments does not by itself fix energy-area cells that collapse in float64.

Controls before inference: massless regression, isolated decay (number plus total energy), detailed balance, Pauli limits with explicit degeneracies, and abundance under mesh refinement. A stable nonrelativistic companion should be counted in matter, not assigned a constant Delta N_eff. Early x0=.005 full CLASS hierarchy remains a separate open validation. This checkpoint does not change the published research conclusions.

## Projection reference: completed in the cloud
In a seeded sample of 50,000 synthetic isotropic on-shell two-body decays, with daughter-to-parent mass ratios 0.08 and 0.18, depositing each daughter in two neighboring shell-energy means conserved each daughter's number and total pair energy on three grids. Maximum relative event-level mismatch was 4.0e-16. Single-shell deposition had 6.64%, 3.65%, and 1.57% RMS relative pair-energy mismatch on 48, 96, and 192 cells respectively. These are errors for this synthetic sample only. On the initial tight 48-cell grid 30 daughter events lay outside the shell-mean support; that experiment failed rather than silently clipping the events. The enlarged upper grid fixed coverage for the sample. For a separate near-rest control, the true rest energy lay below the first cell mean, so positive linear interpolation could not conserve energy. There is no guarantee of coverage from using momentum edges alone.

## Boundary moment ledger

On the original 48-cell grid, the 30 daughter energies outside the interpolation-node range were still inside the physical grid. A diagnostic ledger keeps their count and energy separately (rather than clipping or extrapolating the occupations). Across 50,000 events its energy residual divided by absolute incident energy flux was 1.57e-16 for positive weights and 1.35e-17 for a synthetic signed source. The signed low-edge control has zero net count and nonzero transferred energy, so a count-only ledger is insufficient; energetic events outside the physical grid are still rejected. See [evidence](results/pair-boundary-moments-reference-2026-11-26.json).

Reproduce: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python kinetics/test_pair_boundary_moments_reference.py`.

This diagnostic is not a boundary occupation or a collision operator. A real solver must control the impact of boundary residuals on inverse reactions and Pauli blocking; next test whether adaptive boundary refinement makes the unresolved *absolute* flux negligible in physically motivated forward/reverse rate samples, before treating the ledger as an error estimate in an FRW run.
