# A small hypothetical bare mass changes the frozen local source before the aperture closes

The earlier [bare scalar stress audit](INDEPENDENT-EARLY-POLE-DAUGHTER-BARE-SCALAR-STRESS-2026-11-27.md)
computed kinematic reach, not rates. This separate diagnostic
uses the original initial cold occupations and both prepared
hard states. Insert the same hypothetical bare mass in both
arms, re-solve the scalar pole at that fixed cold seed, and
evaluate the allowed cold decay/inverse-decay packets. Do not
advance the system, add a physical bare mass calibration, or
change the outside hard occupations. The zero-bare-mass
packet source reproduces the original initialized drift.
The paired receipt reports spontaneous and stimulated parts
separately inside and outside the selected daughter interval.

On hard depth four with 32 cold nodes, the paired rate is
-0.01223583 at zero bare mass, +0.00012801 at a bare mass
0.1 times the initial massless *effective* gap, and +0.00224017
at 0.2 times that gap. This compares two frozen source
evaluations, not two evolved cold populations. Within the
first sampled negative-to-positive bracket, the root of this
frozen contrast is .09950 at depth four. At the original
hard depth it is .11721 (32 modes) and .11720 (78 nodes).
This does not locate every possible sign change outside
those sampled brackets.

Removing the change in dressed hard geometry and keeping
only the prepared daughter occupations gives a vanishing
paired change once the original aperture has closed. At
bare-to-effective-gap ratio .257 it gives exactly zero in all
three fixtures; the fully reconstructed paired effect there is
only about +1.8e-9, from the change of dressed geometry and
outside packets. At ratio 3.13 no represented packets remain
in these three original hard fixtures. This is not an exclusion
of other momenta, collisions or physical models.

Run `early_pole_daughter_bare_scalar_frozen_source.py` on the
original hard/cold32 and refined hard/cold32 archives and the
original-hard angular initializer. Use the published common
angular mesh with `--angular-mesh` for the last control.
