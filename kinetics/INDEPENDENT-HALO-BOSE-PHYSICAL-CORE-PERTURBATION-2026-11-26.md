# Why changing the core normalization matters for the observable signal

The [F583-4 sampling test](INDEPENDENT-HALO-F583-4-CORE-PROJECTION-2026-11-26.md)
gives a much smaller rounded-core contrast when
the physical mass and velocity scale are held
fixed than when both models are forced to share
the same dimensionless central-density scale.
Here check the small physical difference without
subtracting two independently rounded rotation
curves.

Use the critical physical scale $R$ for **both**
Poisson equations and denote the critical density
integral at the origin by $I_c$. Their source
functions differ explicitly only while the capped
potential is below $\delta=\ln(1+1/F)$.
With $\xi$ the common dimensionless radius,
the leading cap deficit in this inner region is
$-\sqrt\delta\,C(\xi^2/(6\delta))/I_c$, where
$$
 C(w)=\int_0^{1-w}\sqrt t\,[(w+t)^{-1}-1]\,dt
 \quad (0\le w\le1).
$$
The higher-potential source is identical as a
function of $y$; the remaining difference
comes from the potential generated by the
small inner perturbation. Interchanging the
order of integration gives
$\int_0^1 C(w)\,dw=8/45$.
In the matching window $\sqrt\delta\ll\xi\ll1$,
the constant potential offset sourced by the inner
deficit is therefore
$-[8/(15 I_c)]\delta^{3/2}$.
The decaying enclosed-mass contribution is of
order $\delta^2/\xi$ there.
The exterior response at fixed positive $\xi$
is the linearized Poisson equation about the
critical profile, seeded by this inner offset.
Thus it is of order $F^{-3/2}$, with nonuniform
corrections near the shrinking inner region.

The numerical control propagates both the
critical potential and a difference variable
scaled by $\delta^{3/2}$. It integrates the
capped source correction directly rather than
subtracting two total densities. The normalized
retained speed-response vector at $F=10^9$
agrees with the leading matched solution to .18%.
At finite $F=7000$, higher corrections are still
large across the smallest recorded radii, so the
leading asymptote is not used to predict the
F583-4 comparison. That finite control gives
$\Delta V/V_0=2.02\times10^{-7}$ at $\xi=.337$
before adding baryons or aperture projection,
consistent with the independent sampled comparison.

This identifies a small-signal cancellation from
the fixed physical normalization of one isothermal
model, not a bound on other halo cores, observations
or the full cosmological prior.
