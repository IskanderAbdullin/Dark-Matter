# Outside compensation changes little in the elastic initial slope

Use native, raw local, compensated local and compensation-only
inputs in the held fixed fine graph. The compensation is the same
retained-number and bare-energy fit used for the finite chart; the
two aperture cells are exact in each arm. The original arms replay
bit-for-bit before comparing the added directional tests.

At the 3e-15 probe the elastic derivative is about -459,567 for
unmatched local and -467,384 for matched local. Outside compensation
alters the native-to-local elastic slope differential by about
0.25%, despite reducing the finite positive mean shift by about
83%. The compensation-only slope shift relative to native is
about -7,508 in the same exposure units. All three derivative
probes resolve these shifts. These are component derivatives at
the initial state; they neither establish a physical attribution
nor explain the finite interaction on their own.

The same static experiment can be reproduced with the paired archive
and `early_pole_daughter_initial_aperture_flux_reference.py
--include-retuned`.
