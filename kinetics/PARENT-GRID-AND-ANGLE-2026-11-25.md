# Parent-shell aliasing, guarded steps and an independent parent-angle kernel

Checkpoint continuation after d49f614, 2026-11-25.

## Defect and first remedy
The original daughter-rectangle area is projected by representative daughter energy onto adjacent parent energy shells. A 251-point geometric scan in x=.1..5 on n=120 shows a worst parent-shell spontaneous load / physical shell width of 12542.75 at x=.604681, shell [.0008773,.0015099]. At x=.2 the ratio is 64.63. Total equilibrium-weighted rate can be nearly correct while individual shell rates are grossly wrong. Uniform parent mesh [0,24] + logarithmic tail reduces worst ratio to 3.60; balanced mesh [0,2] finer gives 51.07. The artifact is assignment of a finite daughter rectangle area to a tiny parent cell, not a physical rate enhancement.

The legacy BDF failure is reproduced at x=2.62175: parent shell index 1 jumps from .03703 to -.04605 at an accepted step. Added a step guard that rejects a significantly out-of-domain accepted occupation and restarts from the last valid state with a smaller first step. It does NOT clip/renormalize occupation. Small floating roundoff below 1e-7 is tolerated; this is a monitored restart strategy, not a proof of unconditional positivity. Legacy guarded result reaches x=3, but cross-kernel closure source mismatch 1.274% fails the 1% handoff criterion. Balanced and coarse variants can also fail the cross-kernel guard; no completion asserted there.

With published Laine rates and uniform parent grid, kappa=.006 n=120/200 passes late handoff and gives Omega=.1023250/.1046068. n=240 at kappa=.012 gives .1253781. All use the old shared daughter log grid and remain mesh-dependent.

## Independent kernel
New parent_angle_kernel.py constructs the scalar energy interval for each parent representative, integrates nonnegative nodal hat basis functions exactly, and maps E2=E1-Ephi to adjacent fermion energy representatives with fractional stoichiometry. Each parent-shell total rate cannot exceed its physical time-dilated vacuum rate. The projection preserves discrete number and energy, and geometric occupation/blocking interpolation preserves detailed balance at positive occupations. Independent heavy fermion momentum grid avoids assigning large event flux into tiny low-q fermion cells. Nodal hats spread support near angular boundaries and out-of-range weight is dropped. Therefore this is an independent discretization, not an exact continuum solution.

At n=120 floor 1e-12 distorts late x=20 balance by 2.81e-4, while exact floor=0 gives 2.24e-15 and floor 1e-18 gives 3.55e-10; change independent default to 1e-18 and check exact solution. Complete x=80 angle results at .006: n80 .1181130, n120 .11649284 (exact and regularized agree at <1e-8 relative), n240 .11543645 (uniform heavy mesh .11543869). n120 .012 gives .13492860. Shared daughter grid angle attempt fails with minimum step spacing: do not use it. n240 equilibrium-weighted worst shell loss 3.69% is late-time and not a certified production error; must check time dependence. These results conflict with old shared-grid model at same width; separate kernel vs mesh effects before selecting a fit.

## Independent analytic benchmark
For massless scalar and thermal FD parent, empty daughters, eta=1-r^2, E1_min=q/eta+eta*x^2/(4*q). dN_phi/dlnx/dq = g*x*kappa*x^2/(2*pi^2*eta) log(1+exp(-E1_min)), integrated in each scalar cell by 64-point Gauss-Legendre. Across x=.1,.2,.3,.5,1,2,5, n480 max peak-growth relative bias is .1280% for old rectangle/shared, .01565% for rectangle/separate, .33854% for angle/separate. L1 number error .1711%, .06566%, .30469%. n240 max peak errors .518%, .0874%, 1.2015%. The separate rectangle is the best on this local analytic screen; compare both full kernels under grid refinement.

Sources for positivity schemes: Torlo, Oeffner & Ranocha, "Issues with positivity-preserving Patankar-type schemes", Applied Numerical Mathematics 182 (2022), doi:10.1016/j.apnum.2022.07.014; warns of oscillation and order loss near zero, so no blind use of a Patankar scheme.
