In the strictly interior domain, dS/dt=Σw_a (F−R)ln(F/R)≥0. The vector field points inward at f=0 and f=1 and at b=0. This does NOT ensure an unrestricted explicit Euler time step preserves bounds. At common T, chemical equilibrium requires μ1=μ2+μφ. Disconnected event graphs can have extra spurious invariants, so their equilibria do not prove thermalization.
