# Direct cubic mediator: restricted stability and radiative matching

The minimal positive-portal renormalizable polynomial with a globally nonnegative quartic fails a combined stability and threshold screen. A restricted-field version remains open and has a much smaller primordial contact interaction than a constant copy of the original stabilizing square. Both statements are conditional on the specific coefficient bounds below.

## Global quartic test

Use V4/χ⁴ = λA y²/24 + λS h⁴/24 + λχ/4 + hSχ h²/4 + (hAχ + hAS h²)y/4 − |κ|hy/2, where h=|S/χ| and y=A²/χ²≥0. Here χ is the trigger, S is the mediator, κ=g0/v≈7.93e-31 and the late field value sets the cubic g0. The chosen sign of S makes the mixed term negative. The eta field can be zero along the tested direction.

To allow the small primordial contact rate, take even the generous λA≤λ(σ/m=1e-7 cm²/g)≈1.08e-11. The previous [frozen-spectrum calculation](CONTACT-ELASTIC-AND-CONDENSATE-LIMITS-2026-11-25.md) already finds significant elastic change at this cross section. Impose finite matching-scale sensitivity of the A quartic from S loops ≤λA cap, 3hAS² ln(2)/(16π²), and individual finite trigger mass thresholds ≤μ² at Q=M or Q=mS, hAχ M²/(32π²) and hSχ mS²/(32π²). Allow λS up to 4π to favor stability. The finite quartic/logarithmic formula follows from differentiating the one-loop scalar effective potential with m²(φ)=m²+hφ²/2.

At h=(6λχ/λS,max)^(1/4), set all positive portals at their caps and minimize the remaining quadratic in y. It is negative. The single explicit direction requires λA≥7.38e-6, about **682,000×** the generous cap. Reducing any of those nonnegative coefficients cannot fix that witness. This is a failure of global tree-quartic positivity within the specified bounds. The masses dominate this witness until χ≈3021 reduced Planck masses, so it would be incorrect to infer an instability in the modeled range of χ. Running, counterterm cancellations, other symmetry structures and new fields can change the screen. For scalar threshold conventions see [Martin](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0111209).

## Finite-field alternative

Set the extra mixed portals to zero for this tree check and keep a small positive S quartic. At fixed χ, write g=|κχ|. For S≤M²/g, the effective quadratic in A is positive. For S>M²/g, minimize in A² and compare the negative term to the positive λS S⁴/24. The maximum of (g S−M²)²/S⁴ is g⁴/(16M⁴), attained at S=2M²/g. Thus λA≥9(κχ_cap)⁴/(4λS M⁴) suffices for the A/S sector to have its minimum at A=S=0 for every |χ|≤χ_cap. Dropping the positive S mass makes this a conservative bound. The reference trajectory peaks at χ≈0.00484 reduced Planck masses. Choose χ_cap=0.01 reduced Planck masses and λS=3.42e-6 (10% of vacuum detuning in the illustrative one-loop S mass threshold). The sufficient constant **λA=1.67e-21** is about 5 million times below the constant quartic that would reproduce the term in the original stabilizing square at peak χ. Stability all the way to a reduced Planck mass under the same bound would require λA≈1.67e-13.

For both saved production spectra, a conditional rearrangement bound on the isolated small constant A quartic gives comoving integrated change in L1/Nref <1.29e-4 and integrated positive mode gain below 7.6e6 for occupation cap 2.1 f_peak≈2.15e10. It assumes total number ≤2Nref, positive A quasiparticle mass ≥86 eV, tree elastic kinetics, and H≥0.5 H0 sqrt(Or) r². The bound uses the smaller of the relativistic and nonrelativistic inverse-energy moments, and analytically integrates the resulting envelope over all scale factors. The peak Hartree gap-squared ratio from this contact term alone is below 1.3e-12. The same bound is uninformative for the square-equivalent constant quartic or the reduced-Planck-field choice. The number and occupation caps must still be justified for production, and scattering with other species, coherence and interference are separate checks.

The constant quartic is χ independent and removes the χ-dependent A figure-eight term used in the original two-loop vacuum screen. The relaxed S tadpole and sunset remain. The maximum finite trigger threshold ratio across Q/M=.5,1,2 drops from .7515 to .6715. This scale-local comparison does not establish radiative matching to a high-scale production theory.
