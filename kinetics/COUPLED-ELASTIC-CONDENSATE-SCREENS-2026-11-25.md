## Zero-mode cutoff trap
For fixed positive on-shell m and regular isotropic integrable f(K), K=E-m, the one-zero-leg tree lambda phi^4/4! gain-minus-loss integrand is f(b)f(c)-f(b+c)(1+f(b)+f(c)). Two-body phase space yields lambda^2/(128 pi^3 m) times the integral in db dc, matching dilute evaporation cross section. With no lower cutoff, the quadratic terms integrate to exactly the same (integral f)^2; seed zero-mode evaporation is negative, -integral K f dK. This cancellation assumes regular integrability and does NOT extend to the singular distribution at condensation. For cutoff K>=k, the leftover positive strip is 2 integral da f(a) integral_{max(k,a-k)}^a f(b) db, while spontaneous loss is integral_{2k}^infty (a-2k)f(a) da. Evaluate strip directly to avoid cancellation; exact piecewise constant integration checked by analytic box and subdivisions.
