# Variational entropy-energy and particle-count gates for the isolated thermal benchmark (2026-11-24 UTC)

## Scope and assumptions
Minimal ideal/quasiparticle dark gas: real scalar m=86 eV (g=1), Dirac particle+antiparticle m2=20 MeV and m1=100 MeV (g=4 each). Assume initial **actual thermal** common SM/dark T=0.6882487043 GeV, zero chemical potentials, g*S,SM=61.75 as an illustrative value inherited from the electron transport proxy, **not** a demonstrated decoupling temperature. Subsequently assume no net dark entropy export, SM comoving entropy conserved, homogeneous FRW kinetic entropy with quantum occupations and positive quasiparticle energy; no extra light fields, binding-energy-dominated phase or substantial interaction corrections. Correlations beyond the ideal gas are not included. A nonthermal initial population, continued entropy exchange or new light degrees can evade this result. Do not equate the conditional bound to an actual thermal rate, relic, or BBN likelihood.

## 1. Variational bound independent of internal thermalization
For each mode and quantum statistics, maximizing S-beta E gives the zero-chemical-potential FD/BE occupation. Equivalently the nonnegative relative entropy inequality gives
    s_actual <= rho_actual/T + P_eq(T, mu=0)/T
for EVERY trial T>0. If isolated quantum kinetic evolution has nonnegative comoving entropy production (or collisionless evolution preserves it), then s_actual >= r*s_SM, where r = g*S,d(Tdec)/g*S,SM(Tdec). Therefore
    rho_actual >= sup_{T>0} [T*r*s_SM-P_eq(T)].
The stationary point s_eq(T)=r*s_SM yields rho_eq, the minimum energy at specified entropy, whether or not actual f is thermal, asymmetric, frozen-out, or contains a zero-entropy massive condensate. This is a lower bound, not an evolution law. Finite-mass pressure includes both heavy species; do not remove them by assumption. Trial T may be unrelated to kinetic temperature. In the scalar relativistic / heavy Boltzmann suppressed limit this becomes rho_min/rho_nu,1=(4/7)(10.75*r)^(4/3), with Tref defined via s_SM=(2*pi^2/45)*10.75*Tref^3. Tref approximately equals neutrino T for ideal SM instantaneous decoupling, especially after e+e- reheating; an actual neutrino-transport calculation is needed for precision.

## 3. Relic and a stronger count-constrained gate
Today, s_D >= r*2891.2 cm^-3 yields sharp ideal-gas thermodynamic energy lower bound Omega_D h^2 >= 0.08372118 (trial optimal T=2.42133 eV). This does **not** exclude observed 0.120 by entropy alone. In contrast, if all this entropy becomes a zero-chemical-potential relativistic real scalar which then freezes collisionlessly without further entropy or number transfer, s/n=2*pi^4/(45*zeta(3)) and n0=103.8931 cm^-3 gives Omega_phi h^2=0.84790594 even before a heavy contribution. To match 0.120 in this particular thermal-freezeout branch requires r<=0.01831615 (14.1525% retained, Tdark/TSM<=0.52113 at fixed initial g*S,d). The actual thermal history and scalar spectrum are unproved.

Independent additional constraint if the total comoving number of dark particles does not decrease after a BBN epoch and all surviving particles have mass >=86 eV: today's abundance cap 0.120 implies n_BBN/Tref^3 <= nmax=0.023981035873565558, while s_BBN/Tref^3>=0.6102760337968306, or s/n>=25.4483. Thus rho>=T*(s_min+ln(z)*nmax)-P_upper, even if real state has separate species chemical potentials and nonthermal f. A single explicit trial z=4.84319e-10, T/Tref=378.673 gives rho/rho_nu,1 >= **47.31917**. The trial is a mathematical parameter, not a physical temperature; if extra states become accessible at that trial T, the chosen nine-state bound no longer applies and must be extended. For looser BBN cap of one neutrino energy, this inequality inverted implies n_BBN/Tref^3 >=0.0596153, at least 2.48594 times today's maximum comoving count. At least 59.77% net particle-number reduction would be necessary after BBN if high entropy remains, even for that generous cap. For two/five-neutrino caps the corresponding minimum destruction fractions are 47.39%/32.73%. No assumption of number conservation is proven for the full network: inverse 1<->2, portal annihilation, higher-order scalar number-changing reactions can alter count.
