## 2. Direct cubic activation without H
New potential:
V=M^2 A^2/2 + mS^2/2*[S+g0*(chi/v)*A^2/(2mS^2)]^2 + V_chi,eta.
The square is positive and provides lambda_A(x)=3g0^2*x^2/mS^2. At x=0 neither the cubic nor stabilizing quartic exists. S remains below 2M, so no vacuum inverse decay; no H conversion. Here sigma_LL(v,t)=sigma0*x^4*[a/(a+w^2)]^2 to dominant s-channel accuracy. g0^2=lambda_from_sigma(sigma0)*a*m^2(vT/c)^2; g0 only a few micro-eV. The trigger parameters are renormalized inputs, not a constructed high-scale EFT.

Near mS=2M the Martin two-loop MS scalar sunset I(M^2,M^2,4M^2)/M^2=-4 LA LS+LA^2+4 LA+8 LS-15, LA=log(M^2/Q^2), LS=log(4M^2/Q^2). At Q=M this is 8 log 4-15. The chi-dependent vacuum coefficient is g0^2/(16pi^2)^2*[3 A(M^2)^2/(8mS^2)-A(M^2)^2/(8mS^2)-I/4], where the second term is the relaxed S one-loop tadpole contribution and A(x)=x(log(x/Q^2)-1). Evaluate 2*abs(V2)/X, X=mu^2 v^2, at Q=M/2,M,2M. Finite threshold comparison is not a renormalization invariant naturalness theorem; explicit counterterms, UV matching and symmetry matter. The stabilizing dimension-6 interaction can still generate power-sensitive vacuum terms, and a naive Wilsonian figure-8 cutoff is only ~181-253 eV in the refined examples. The pole mass relation is tuned at relative level ~4e-8 to 9e-8, even if small g0 makes low-energy mass corrections small. No solution of the high-scale production/matching naturalness issue.
