# Resolve native power under two abundance normalizations and a cooled shape control

Use the distinct [abundance choices](INDEPENDENT-WARM-DIRECT-ABSOLUTE-NORMALIZATION-2026-11-26.md)
in native CLASS. The early-start comparisons have the
same present total dark density, exact sampled k modes,
and a same-PSD negligible trace for each comparison.
They are post-production linear models. The missing
stable density in the absolute scalar case is independently
provided by CDM, not generated by the portal.

The absolute scenario uses omega_scalar=.000158975
(or 0.13248% of the assumed total .12), preserving the
numerical direct vacuum yield without the factor-631
rescaling applied to the held composition. At k=10
the resulting decrease in P, rather than ln P, is .880%,
and at k=2 it is .498%. At z=20 the corresponding k=10
Delta ln P is −.008408. The figures above are the dense
120-q/lmax40 exact-mode result. A separate compact
normalization using the conditional yield upper but
the *same shape* gives Delta ln P≈−.012516 at k=10,
z=5; it is not a bound on all source spectral shapes.
The literal compact absolute run is not counted here:
one log was empty during a temporary disk limit, and
that specific comparison is being repeated. The dense
power tables and density log were verified independently.

A separate illustration imposes 10% scalar abundance
on the same warm template, without deriving that value
from the production. It leaves P/P_trace≈.5034 at k=10,
z=5, with no half-power crossing at the sampled points.
It is not a prediction, constraint or substitute for
an independent production history.

## A physical entropy event would also change the momentum unit

The earlier algebraic rescaling of the composition
left q/T_gamma unchanged. A uniform visible-sector
entropy increase by D for already decoupled particles
would instead shrink it by D^(-1/3). For D≈631 this
is a factor .11658, and the shell-bounded mean path
from the same archive to equality moves from
[8.371,9.272] to [1.452,1.624] Mpc. At fixed degrees
of freedom, achieving this instantaneously would imply
about 5,413 times the radiation energy at fixed a;
this is a scaling illustration, not a feasible mechanism
or a model of the intervening expansion.

If only the final momentum unit is changed and the
same small rescaled abundance retained, the resulting
compact native response at k=10, z=5 is −6.580e−6
relative to its own cooler trace, about 47.1% of
the original compact rescaled result. At k=2 it is
−1.260e−6, rather than −7.852e−6. This cleanly separates
an algebraic count rescaling at fixed spectrum from
the colder shape one would get in a simple entropy
change. It does not solve any entropy release, changes
in neutrino temperature, or observational viability.
