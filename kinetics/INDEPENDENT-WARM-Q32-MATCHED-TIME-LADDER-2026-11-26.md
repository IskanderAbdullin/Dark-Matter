# A coupled four-level time ladder on unchanged q32 support

The [matched q32/q64 comparison](INDEPENDENT-WARM-MATCHED-EXPLICIT-SUPPORT-COUPLED-2026-11-26.md)
found a support difference much smaller than the two-to-four
time refinement. Refine the time grid further with **identical**
q32 momenta, initial actual spectrum, known exterior gravity,
event quadratures, operators and final expansion (b=1.001).
Keep two, four, eight and sixteen accepted outer blocks.

Successive common-shell number (L^1) differences, each
normalized to its initial species number, are (5.21,,2.66,,
1.35) in units of (10^{-5}) for scalars, and (3.08,,1.55,,
.779) in the same units for light fermions. Full energy and
pressure show the same near-halving. Across all tested moments
the ratio of successive differences is 1.96–1.99, consistent
with first-order temporal convergence of this scheme in the
sampled interval. This is not a continuum bound.

The new one-way exterior birth energy changes by .0548%,
.0292% and .0150% on successive doublings, a separate
diagnostic from shared retained number. Inherited cohort
fields are checked against their original archived values
with exact expected rebase factors; combined retained plus
newly received counts are independently conserved. The
rest-mode light occupation is about (5.8041	imes10^{-5})
for every ladder member, monitored separately from shell norms.

The matched q32/q64 difference remained smaller than even
the 8-to-16 time difference. That finding prioritizes time
resolution on this short interval; it does not rule out
later sensitivity to wider momentum support or effects from
missing physical channels.
