# Soft two-state late activation and derivative production: conditional candidate, new bottlenecks

2026-11-25. Continuation of [velocity resonance and direct activation](VELOCITY-RESONANCE-AND-DIRECT-ACTIVATION-2026-11-25.md). This is a deliberately limited consistency study, not a complete microscopic theory, cosmological likelihood or rotation-curve fit.

## 1. Potential and hierarchy

Test V=.5 m^2 A^2+.5(m^2+Delta)B^2+kappa chi A B+lambda_B B^4/24+lambda_chi(chi^2-v^2)^2/4+g_eta^2 chi^2 sum eta_i^2/2. At chi=0 A has no tree B quartic. Define x=chi/v, delta=kappa v, light eigenstate B probability u=(1-Delta/sqrt(Delta^2+4 delta^2 x^2))/2; lambda_LL=lambda_B u^2. The trace of the mass matrix is constant, unlike a direct quartic switch. Choose u=(3-sqrt(3))/4 to minimize delta for a given target lambda_LL and illustrative Delta=lambda_B m^2/(32 pi^2).

For sigma_LL/m=.1 cm2/g, m=86 eV: lambda_B=1.077e-7, Delta=2.522e-6 eV^2, delta=3.206e-6 eV^2, vacuum splitting at full mixing ~4.19e-8 eV. X=mu^2 v^2, illustrative log loop threshold delta^2/(16 pi^2)=6.51e-14 eV^4; thermal eta loop cap pi^2/9 Neta Td^4 with Delta N_eff=.1 at nominal z=5 is 1.964e-13. This gives an overlap ratio ~3, not a symmetry-protected prediction. The unsuppressed Wilsonian quadratic splitting threshold, if applied, would permit Lambda_B/m only ~1.32 at these parameters (and ~2 at z8 for sigma=.1). Generic high-scale mass degeneracy is therefore not natural; require a low-scale UV origin or explicit protection. Different counterterms/matching choices are not predictions of the same fixed UV model. An exact symmetry rotating an interaction basis and its radiatively generated mass splitting together avoids orientation-dependent vacuum energy, but the inert eigenstate then follows an adiabatic homogeneous rotation; merely rotating the basis does not activate its self-scattering. Rapid walls or nonadiabatic conversion require a separate model.

## 2. Dynamical switch and loop effects

Integrate homogeneous chi in fixed LCDM H, free-streaming initially massless Bose eta bath (chosen Delta N_eff, not derived), adiabatic light eigenstate medium using frozen scalar PSD; early negative medium susceptibility is only an adiabatic diagnostic, not true flavor evolution. E-folds are a local diagnostic, not halo formation.

Include explicit eta one-loop Coleman-Weinberg term with two choices: preserve origin curvature and vev, or preserve vev and its curvature. Scan 20 cases at X=1.05 times the illustrative loop floor, seed 1e-8. For .1,z5,mu/H100: integrated frozen relative change N(q>2 peak)=-.197%/-.109%, halo diagnostic 1.18/1.07. For .3,z8,mu/H30: -.699%/-.542%, 3.48/3.50. For 1.25,z8,mu/H60: -5.29%/-.483%, 15.84/10.55, demonstrating strong scheme sensitivity near the loop cap. These are actual roll solutions, frozen spectral estimates and conditional local collision counts; not a proven viable window. The small extra energy is not a cosmological likelihood. Independently integrate comoving energy/work for the .1 z5 mu/H100 cases with exact eta energy/pressure and first-order light mass shift: max residual relative to represented comoving energy excursion falls from 5.95e-6/2.94e-6 at rtol2e-7 to 1.99e-7/8.46e-8 at rtol2e-9. Nonlinear roll, domain walls and metric/bath fluctuations still require a coupled perturbation treatment. The maximum nominal early tachyonic band in the output includes the approximate early adiabatic medium; do not identify it with a physical instability spectrum.

## 3. Early conversion and initial conditions

For seed 1e-8 the frozen final A PSD extrapolation gives u~1.6e-16, integrated conservative direct AA heavy-mode gain <4.4e-10, heavy-number source <1.5e-20 if f_H<=1, and catalytic LH->HH count <.0012 per heavy particle under that same condition. For seed 1e-4 the corresponding bounds are enormous; the tiny initial dilute count alone cannot establish safety. This is not a bootstrap bound on the full heavy distribution, and ignores early thermal level crossings, prior B population and coherent mixing. At late z~3 the original frozen A kinetic energy per particle is ~1e-12 eV versus minimum gap 1.47e-8 eV, so instantaneous conversion at fixed gap is energetically limited to ~1e-4 fraction (excludes field work); halo kinetic energies are very different.

Independent radiation-era coherent cosine calculation for A (F=3.49214e10 GeV) gives additional abundance ~3.31% at initial angle 1, ~.776% at .5, 114% at 3 rad. To leave <1% in small-angle coherent DM requires |theta_i| roughly <.57; no demonstrated initial condition. An independent quartic B oscillator action calculation, neglecting fragmentation, gives 1% coherent abundance at initial amplitude ~8.55e14 GeV; action's late oscillation span ~2% on base run (refinement recorded). For the particular .1,z5,mu/H100 trigger, 60-e-fold free random walk reaches the chosen chi seed at H_inf~2.29e6 GeV (per-log ~1.77e7 GeV). Low-scale inflation could be compatible with the minimum instantaneous reheating H~1e-6 GeV, but no inflation/reheating/isocurvature model has been constructed. A decoupled initial eta distribution is assumed, not dynamically produced.

## 4. Derivative production and thermal consistency

Derivative current (d_mu A/F) bar N2 gamma^mu(cV+cA gamma5) N1+h.c. gives Gamma=M1^3(1-r^2)^3/(16 pi F^2) at mA=0. F=3.49214e10 GeV reproduces kappa=.00685 in the existing width convention (F=2 f/C). Independent gamma-matrix traces reproduce exact finite-mass vector/axial formulas to <5e-16 relative. Shift symmetry can protect a mass in an EFT, unlike extrapolating a lone on-shell pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction to loops. Explicit breaking by the late mixing and the UV theory still require a consistent construction. For a periodic cosine potential lambda_A=-m^2/F^2 is very small. A high-T massless Maxwell-Boltzmann sterile field-redefinition contact scattering benchmark gives additional hot abundance fraction 1.23e-10 for reheating T=860 TeV; even at T=F the estimate is 5e-6 (factor12 sensitivity 6e-5), but EFT-edge extrapolation and missing full channels are not rigorous bounds.

Massless-A tree forward amplitude for either pure derivative current is -8 D (p.k)^2/[F^2(D^2-4(p.k)^2)] (D=Mi^2-Mj^2); independent spin traces agree to ~2e-15. Use real principal value at the intermediate pole and leading-density shift -integral f M/(2E). For actual scalar snapshots the largest sampled heavy squared-mass-gap correction is ~2.5e-8 of the vacuum gap. For scalar dispersion, a rescaled FD shape is not sufficient for tiny A/B splitting. Added optional dense PSD history of BOTH heavy species. Independent actual n240 rerun reached x=12.45 before its 240 s wall limit (not a new final abundance); use it only for the early thermal snapshots. Actual heavy PSD principal-value result max |Pi_A/k_peak^2|~1.92e-8, angular quadrature discrepancy <=7.87e-12 of k_peak^2. The tiny B gap is crossed near x=.137 and 1.513 in this specific approximation. Sparse/secant LZ exponents for seed 1e-8 are ~7.5e-58 and 9.2e-49, suggesting extremely nonadiabatic conversion, but not a bound: thermal widths, thermal spin spectra, damping, exact momentum-resolved crossing and potential higher-order terms matter. FD integration produced a convergence warning on a surrogate sample; actual PSD result uses independent split-shell quadratures. No self-consistent finite-temperature production calculation claimed.

## 5. Halo gas must be rederived

The B quartic induces LL->LH and LL->HH, not merely elastic LL. At full mixing and original critical F583-4 V0=44.1 km/s, gap/T=.0215, so both channels are open. For initial L critical Bose gas and H empty, exact NR angular integration with final L Bose factor gives initial H source ~17.10 n sigma_LL v_T (48/96/144 quadratures agree within ~.4%). The frozen 10 Gyr extrapolation is ~22.55, manifestly not a physical fraction; it shows the initial composition cannot be assumed fixed. An isolated ideal gas at fixed initial total number and kinetic+internal energy equilibrates at T/T_initial=.779, heavy fraction=.476 and no condensate. For V0=10 km/s, the same box model instead permits ~5.57% condensate, demonstrating temperature dependence. No time-dependent chemical equilibrium has been proven.

At original F583-4 mass, density, temperature and R,V0, an isothermal two-state chemical equilibrium requires alpha=-mu/T=.193 rather than the one-state critical alpha=0; the central cusp is absent and circular speed differs by up to 14.4% over xi<=12. If instead both are evaluated at the light critical point, critical number density increases ~1.82-fold and the simple fixed-density mass inference would shift toward ~74 eV (not an observational fit). The original 86 eV calibration and halo relaxation inference cannot be carried over unchanged. Refit with actual two-state collision/Poisson evolution, finite halo and baryons; isolated fixed-density profiles are not rotation-curve constraints.
