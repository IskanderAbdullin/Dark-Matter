# Frozen transverse statistical source: massive soft limit and exact free cut

This extends the archived collisionless transverse retarded check. It isolates the statistical source generated by free charged quasiparticles, not a population of on-shell photons. Both massive charged species are taken from the frozen fine and third archive endpoints; antiparticles mirror the charges, with the same unit-magnitude gauge charges as in the retarded benchmark.

## Soft limit

Set $u=\omega/k$ with $0<u<1$ and $E=\sqrt{p^2+m^2}$. The angular resonance $\omega=kv_z$ restricts the radial integral to $p\ge p_0=mu/\sqrt{1-u^2}$. Removing the common constants, the transverse symmetrized weight is

```text
S = integral (p^3/E - u^2 p E) f(1-f) dp
D = integral (p^3/E - u^2 p E) (-df/dE) dp
  = 2 (1-u^2) integral p f dp.
```

The second expression follows from integration by parts after converting to energy: the weight in energy is $(1-u^2)E^2-m^2$, zero at the resonant threshold. The boundary at infinity vanishes; histogram jumps enter the integration-by-parts expression exactly. In each occupied bin the primitive of the statistical weight is $(H-L)[(1-u^2)(H^2+HL+L^2)/3-m^2]$ with clipped energy edges $L,H$. An independent momentum quadrature checks that primitive, and the dissipative measure reconstructs the pre-existing transverse retarded imaginary coefficient. The combined-energy ratio is $\sum S/\sum D$, not an unweighted average of species ratios. For a common Fermi temperature it equals $T$; the maximum relative equilibrium defect in the sweep is $7.26\times10^{-13}$.

Near $u=.0047$ the fine/third values are 1.01276/1.01356 in the archive's energy units. Near $u=.9996$ they are about .92585/.92787. A constant noise-to-dissipation ratio is not supported by these frozen states. For context, see [Carrington, Hou and Thoma, hep-ph/9708363](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9708363) for the ultrarelativistic nonequilibrium hard-loop framework; the finite-mass histogram benchmark here is separate.

## Free finite recoil

Let $K=(\omega,0,0,k)$, $s=k^2-\omega^2>0$, and take the incoming and outgoing charged four-momenta $P,P+K$ on an equal-mass shell. The on-shell energy constraint fixes $p_z=(\omega E-s/2)/k$, hence $E\ge (k\sqrt{1+4m^2/s}-\omega)/2$. The nonnegative spin-summed weight, averaged over the two transverse directions, is $M(E)=4p_\perp^2+2s$. The two-body measure after the angular delta function is proportional to $dE/k$. Use the weight with the following three integrands:

```text
G: f(E)   [1-f(E+w)]
L: f(E+w) [1-f(E)]
D: f(E) - f(E+w)
```

Resolve the incoming and shifted outgoing bin boundaries separately. The branch-local weight is quadratic in energy and integrated by Gauss–Legendre. The independently accumulated $D$ agrees with $G-L$ without needing to subtract two nearly equal totals. Positivity of $D$ is tested at the sampled archive points; it is not assumed for every arbitrary distribution. The normalization audit contracts explicit on-shell spinors with two independent transverse polarizations, matches $M(E)$ to $4.45\times10^{-16}$, and verifies $-\operatorname{Im}\Pi_R = g^2 D/(8\pi k)$ for the summed charged species with mirrored antiparticles and the polarization average, to within $9.16\times10^{-6}$ of the soft numerical response over the sampled small scales.

The finite-frequency ratio is $\omega(G+L)/(2D)$. Common thermal Fermi states recover $L/G=\exp(-\omega/T)$ to $4.45\times10^{-16}$, and the exact Bose fluctuation ratio $\omega\coth(\omega/(2T))/2$ to $1.10\times10^{-10}$ with independently quadratured thermal $D$. At archive points $k=.0002,.0008,.00256,.01$, $u=.0047,.25,.9,.99$, finite recoil shifts the statistical ratio from the soft version by at most $8.10\times10^{-5}$ fine and $1.014\times10^{-4}$ third.

## Reproduction and boundary

From `kinetics/` in the PR tree, run:

```sh
python finite_mass_qed_transverse_kinetic_noise_reference.py
python finite_mass_qed_spacelike_exact_statistical_aperture_reference.py
python finite_mass_qed_spacelike_exact_statistical_normalization_audit.py
```

The corresponding JSON files under `results/` record the numerical gates and sampled points. These diagnostics neither determine a collective pole population nor fix an independent homogeneous Keldysh solution. They contain no interacting widths/vertex matching, no resolved-intermediate-state subtraction in the scalar collision term, and no total NLO, abundance, or cosmological prediction.
