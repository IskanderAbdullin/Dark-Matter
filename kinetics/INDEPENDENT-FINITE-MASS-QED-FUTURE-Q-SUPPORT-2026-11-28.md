# Kinematic support beyond the cold scalar bands at fixed transverse k

The [low-q knot audit](INDEPENDENT-FINITE-MASS-QED-SCALAR-Q-KNOT-EXACT-2026-11-28.md) checked the retained region up to q=.0003. This larger audit concerns the same two frozen transverse pole fixtures k=.0015. It enumerates angular endpoint crossings from the known histogram energies, not a physical integrated rate.

Write `T=q−K=P−b`. The photon pole invariant mass exceeds the outgoing scalar mass on both fixtures. If T is timelike, then in the rest frame of K,
`T²=m_s²+μ²−2μE_s^*` implies `E_s^*<μ`. Hence a timelike T is always past-directed, and cannot equal the difference of these future charged on-shell momenta with M>m. For any q>0 the angle excluded by the transfer light cone is therefore determined by `T²≥0`, an affine inequality in the scalar-photon cosine. Its two extreme-cosine null endpoints are about [7.0721e−5, .00180215] (fine) and [6.4406e−5, .00180379] (third). At the midpoint of each interval, the excluded fraction of cosine is .13031 and .13238 respectively. The later crossing E_s=ω does not make T timelike, although the sign of its energy changes.

The charged endpoint calculation now permits both collinear orientations and both signs in the null equation. The first opposite-direction charged shell-edge crossing is around q=.026328 on both archives. Also check interior changes in radial shell ordering: `E_P^edge−E_b^edge=t=E_s−ω` fixes q directly, after which a collinear support condition determines whether the crossing lies inside the retained angular interval. Exactly three such crossings are found in the two fixtures (one fine, two third); following the two angular edges across each root independently verifies the order reversal. No interior shell-order crossing falls inside the original [1e−6,3e−4] interval.

The archive parent support yields the loose forward energy bound `E_s≤E_P^max+ω−m`; reverse transfer also needs an occupied scalar bin, absent above q=25 in these archives. This supplies a finite integration cap near 31.99934 even for forward final-state population factors that do not vanish with the scalar occupation. The cap is an upper bound, not the true last populated or kinematically allowed shell. Small tail occupation alone does not bound the tail reaction integral.
