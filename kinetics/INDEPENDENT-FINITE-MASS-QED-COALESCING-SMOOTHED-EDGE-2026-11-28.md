# Two coalescing charged poles with a resolved occupation ramp

The [archived step check](INDEPENDENT-FINITE-MASS-QED-COALESCING-HISTOGRAM-EDGE-2026-11-28.md) retains two normalized occupation plateaus. Preserve those plateaus, but replace the step in the diagnostic chart by either a linear ramp or the cubic smoothstep. The support is `[η−σ,η+σ]` with `x=D1/ε`; thus σ is prescribed in units of the diagnostic pole width, not as a physical momentum resolution.

With `I(t;h)=integral_{−∞}^t F(x;h) dx` from the step check, average `I(η+σz;h)` on −1≤z≤1 using density 1/2 for the linear ramp or 3(1−z²)/4 for the cubic. This follows by expressing the ramp as a mixture of displaced steps. The linear result can also be evaluated using the analytic antiderivative of I. Insert the resulting average into `2π w⁺ + (w⁻−w⁺) average(I)`. The opposite imaginary sign multiplies either leading expression by `h²/(h²+4)`; changing the edge profile does not remove the sign dependence.

Because the kernel is symmetric about `x=h/2`, symmetric ramps at a reflected pair of edge centers must give a sum of `2π(w⁻+w⁺)`. We verify this to 1.60e−14 and independently integrate both ramp-weighted rational kernels to 2.76e−14 across 150 combinations of archive, separation, center and width. At fixed scaled σ, replacing the sharp step changes the same-sign leading area by as much as 1.16723 for the linear profile. The two continuous profiles can differ by 0.20133 at the *same* scaled σ. These changes survive scaling ε→0 with h, η and σ fixed; sending σ→0 instead recovers the sharp step. At h=η=0 the exact symmetry makes all three profiles coincide in the leading area, a useful but nongeneric control.

The opposite order is informative: for σ→∞ at fixed h and η, the leading common-sign area tends to π(w⁻+w⁺). Its first correction is π(w⁺−w⁻)(h/2−η)/σ times 1 (linear) or 3/2 (cubic). The first moment of F exists because its tails decay as |x|⁻⁴; its mean is h/2 by reflection. Applying dominated convergence to σ times the difference from the midpoint ramp weight gives the stated coefficient. A separate set of broad ramps reproduces both coefficients to 3.29e−4 relative at σ=512. A ramp held broad relative to ε is therefore distinct from either the sharp or fixed-scaled-width paths when the center and root separation remain O(ε).

A finite-width check uses the actual archived mass, spinor numerators, shell Jacobian, and adjacent occupation values. It changes the occupation function locally by hand to the specified profile; it does not smooth the entire archived distribution. Both imaginary orientations, both profiles and three σ are checked at 24 archive/geometry/ε fixtures (288 smoothed spinor integrals). The maximum absolute discrepancy from the corresponding leading predictions is 1.89e−5 on the finite integration window. For example, at third-archive h=2, η=0, σ=3 the common-sign predictions are 6.75522 (linear) and 6.87813 (cubic), instead of 7.17910 for the step. The opposite-sign predictions are half those values.

From `kinetics/`, run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -W error coalescing_ramp_joint_pilot.py` and compare the [receipt](results/finite-mass-qed-coalescing-smoothed-edge-2026-11-28.json). This is a test of a prescribed local smoothing convention, not an inferred medium line shape, resolution prescription, or matched reaction rate.
