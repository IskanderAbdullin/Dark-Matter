# Pressure of the isolated homogeneous coherent comparison

The coherent limit also has a stress check independent of field
fragmentation. For the energy-matched oscillator in
m₀²φ²/2+λφ⁴/24, evaluate the canonical average over its exact
elliptic-function period. The homogeneous virial identity gives
2⟨K⟩ = ⟨φ V′(φ)⟩ and hence
ρ−3⟨P⟩ = m₀²⟨φ²⟩. Unlike the stationary particle Hartree
identity, this is a **cycle-averaged** statement. The cold
field at large amplitude is still radiation-like in the
background even if it has no spatial gradient.

On the tested snapshots the energy-matched coherent pressure
ratio is close to 1/3. When the quadratic and quartic potential
contributions at the turning amplitude are equal, the numerical
cycle-averaged pressure/energy is 0.1751. An independent
quadrature of the isolated rapid-oscillator action
J=∮π dφ, with a³J conserved adiabatically, gives an expansion
factor of about 1.0–1.13 × 10⁷ from these starting energies
to that chosen amplitude equality. It is not a prediction of
a physical transition time: the sector in the kinetic runs
is still driven, and the coherent field can fragment. No
perturbation sound speed follows from this average pressure.
