## Scientific gap, claim boundaries and predeclared gates

The original model has Dirac χ1,χ2 and ONE REAL Z2-odd 86-eV φ. Exact vectorlike U(1)′ Yukawa invariance forces qχ1=qχ2 if the real φ is uncharged. Thus χ2–SM elastic collisions are required for a nonzero χ1–SM gauge current. But even including χ2 would leave a **second** potentially leading physical omission: the mediator Z′ itself is an on-shell particle whenever mV is not far above the temperature, with pair production/decay and finite stress–energy. It cannot simply be a virtual t-channel propagator without independent evidence of negligible real abundance.

This audit tests **necessary model-completion thresholds**, conditional ideal massive-spin-1 equilibrium energy density, and tree two-body decay coefficients. It is *not* a proof Z′ is actually thermal, a viable parameter region, a computed ΔNeff exclusion, or a physical γ_el or 86-eV relic.

The Goldstone a is eaten; s is another physical scalar degree of freedom. ## Independent conditional massive-vector equilibrium thermodynamics

If the broken-phase massive spin-1 Z′ genuinely reaches ideal thermal equilibrium with chemical potential μV=0, its three physical polarizations yield

    rhoV/T⁴ = 3/(2π²) int_0^infty dq q² eps/[exp(eps)−1],
    PV/T⁴ = 1/(2π²) int dq q⁴/eps/[exp(eps)−1],
    eps=sqrt(q²+(mV/T)²).

Define Δg*ρ=30 rhoV/(π²T⁴), Δg*S=45(rhoV+PV)/(2π²T⁴). This is an **IF thermal** equilibrium upper-type reference (not a rigorous bound for arbitrary nonthermal production, which can overshoot equilibrium), independently reproduced with the alternating-free BE fugacity/Bessel sum:

    rhoV/T⁴ = 3 x²/(2π²) sum_n [3K2(nx)/n² + x K1(nx)/n],
    PV/T⁴ = 3 x²/(2π²) sum_n K2(nx)/n²,
    x=mV/T.
