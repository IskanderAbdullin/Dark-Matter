# The two prepared hard inputs begin with opposite cold-rate trends

The [first-interval ladder](INDEPENDENT-EARLY-POLE-FIRST-WINDOW-DYADIC-32-REFINEMENT-2026-11-27.md) found opposite resolution trends under the two initial hard reconstructions. At those exact starting states, evaluate each hard collision contribution separately and use it as a tangent direction for the scalar cold source. Treat reciprocal decay as a direction in both hard moments and cold counts. Use three symmetric probe offsets near zero, without clipping FD moments. The larger exploratory offset (10^{-12}) fails the unmodified FD fit for the native case and is not reported as a tangent.

| Source derivative with respect to exposure at the prepared start | Native archived steps | Count-conserving continuous |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Unlike screened elastic direction | −2.780e6 | +1.9115e5 |
| Same-flavor screened elastic direction | −7.771e5 | −1.7065e5 |
| Total direction | −3.557e6 | +2.0431e4 |

The remaining four directional contributions (reciprocal decay, photon pair, Compton and flavor conversion) sum to about −83 and −71, respectively. In the native preparation the two elastic terms both initially decrease the cold rate; in the continuous preparation their signs oppose one another. The sum of directional projections agrees with the directly evaluated combined-direction derivative to 2.1e−7 of its largest mode across the three retained probes. This is an initial derivative. A linear extrapolation of these slopes across the whole first macro interval is not valid, and the channel fractions need not persist along the trajectory.

As a separate finite-step ablation, set both elastic channel strengths to zero, keeping reciprocal decay, photon pairs, Compton and flavor conversion. On the same first interval the averages are .07862183008 and .05689138987 at two slow macro steps, both within .023% of their own initial instantaneous sources. The sixteen-step controls agree with these averages to better than 3e−11 absolute. This confirms that the large early readjustment of the joined source is not generated by the slow channels alone. It does not show what would happen in an expanding medium.
