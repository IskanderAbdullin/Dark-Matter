# Primary-publication crosswalk, novelty and priority
## Prior work closer than the original five-draft bibliography implied
1. **Abdullin I. G. & Popov V. A. (2019)**, “Гало бозе-конденсата тёмной материи с невырожденной компонентой” / “Bose-condensed dark matter halos with non-degenerate component”, *Пространство, время и фундаментальные взаимодействия* 2019 issue 1. https://www.stfi.ru/journal/STFI_2019_01/STFI_2019_01_Abdullin.pdf. Mixed-condensate and nondegenerate component studied in an earlier regime. Distinguish 2019's predominantly condensed core from 2021's halo with dominant noncondensed component.
2. **Abdullin I. G. & Popov V. A. (2021)**, “Boson dark matter halos with a dominant noncondensed component”, *JCAP* 2021(11)055; https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2021/11/055. Uploaded LaTeX is the definitive source for the present audit.
3. **Abdullin I. G. (2025)**, “Галактическое гало тёмной материи из лёгких бозонов” / “Dark matter halo from light bosons”, *Пространство, время и фундаментальные взаимодействия*, 2025 issue 1, pp.7–11, https://doi.org/10.17238/issn2226-8812.2025.1.7-11; https://www.stfi.ru/journal/STFI_2025_01/STFI_2025_01_Abdullin.pdf. It already presents the minimum noncondensed halo core radius near the transition, so **minimum-core-radius** is NOT a new result of the current work.
4. **Slepian & Goodman (2012)**, “Ruling out bosonic repulsive dark matter in thermal equilibrium”, *MNRAS* 427 839–849, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21901.x. An important adverse paper for a class of thermally equilibrated repulsive bosons. It examines a model with condensed core and nondegenerate envelope; its exclusion should NOT simply be applied without checking equivalence to the different 2021 proposal, but its logic must be confronted quantitatively.
5. **Phys. Rev. E 105, 034145 (2022)**, “Self-gravitating clusters of Bose-Einstein gas with planar, cylindrical, or spherical symmetry: Gaseous density profiles and onset of condensation”, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.105.034145. Relevant independently to equilibrium, mixed phases and critical behavior.
6. **Chavanis (2025)**, “A review of basic results on the Bose–Einstein condensate dark matter model”, https://doi.org/10.3389/fspas.2025.1538434. Review of repulsion, stability, constraints; many quantitative estimates relate to condensate-dominated states and must be mapped carefully onto the current noncondensed regime.
7. **Rogers et al. (2024)**, high-redshift Lyman-alpha thermal-relic free streaming, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.043511. The 5.7 keV number applies to specified thermal WDM distributions, **not directly** to an 86 eV Bose phase-space distribution.
8. **2025 mixed cold+warm Lyman-alpha** https://doi.org/10.1103/4k29-h99l. Required for any scenario claiming cold and hot fractions of the same dark sector.

## Novelty discipline
The new mathematical *specific* statement “any fixed \(g>0\) rounds the ideal critical central cusp in the Hartree–Fock model” is derived here, but wider Bose-gas literature may already contain analogous results. Test the overlap before journal submission. The original five papers 2–5 remain partly methods/proposals, not five empirically solved problems. A prioritized publication sequence: (i) complete critical interaction asymptotics and consistency checks, (ii) fit all 22 original sources with baryonic/covariance data, (iii) cosmological production+linear perturbations, (iv) finite-mass equilibria and stability, (v) Bose-star kinetics with dynamical formation.
